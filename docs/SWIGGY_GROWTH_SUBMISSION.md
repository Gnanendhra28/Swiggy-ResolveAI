# Swiggy Growth: ResolveAI Submission

**Live Application Link:** https://swiggy-resolve-ai.vercel.app  
**GitHub Repository:** https://github.com/Gnanendhra28/Swiggy-ResolveAI  

---

### 1. Who It’s For
Swiggy Delivery Operations Managers, Restaurant Partners, and Hungry Customers across high-density delivery clusters.

### 2. The Problem
Food delivery delays are handled reactively. When kitchen congestion, weather, or rider shortages stall an order, customers only find out when their meal is already late. This fuels repetitive *"Where is my order?"* support tickets, damages restaurant partner ratings, and causes customer churn before anyone can intervene.

### 3. Our Solution (Swiggy ResolveAI)
ResolveAI is an autonomous order intelligence and early recovery engine. Instead of waiting for complaints, ResolveAI analyzes kitchen prep times, rider transit telemetry, and historical order data to forecast delivery risks 15–20 minutes in advance. When high delay risk is detected, multi-agent AI coordinates an immediate, automated resolution: alerting restaurant staff, dispatching backup delivery partners, and sending proactive customer updates with goodwill vouchers—governed by operational approval safety rules.

### 4. Why It Matters
Proactivity transforms potential cancellations into brand-loyalty moments. By resolving operational bottlenecks before the customer complains, ResolveAI slashes support escalations, protects restaurant reputations, and preserves customer trust when unexpected delays occur.
